Built by a junior doctor, for junior doctors.

Anker started because I was tired of trying to keep track of everything on a folded piece of paper. During a busy shift, you’re constantly adding jobs, crossing things out, answering bleeps and scribbling reminders wherever there’s space.

I wanted something that worked like my paper list, but made it easier to see what was left to do and what needed my attention first.

What started as a weekend prototype has been shaped by weeks of real use on the ward.

Created by Dr Maryam Ghani, a UK junior doctor.

Your patients’ details stay with you.

Anker has no account, no login and no clinical sync. Your information stays on your device rather than being uploaded to an Anker server.

No account No login No clinical sync No patient names
ON YOUR DEVICE

Where it’s stored

On your iPhone, in the app’s own storage. Patients, jobs, notes and bleeps are not uploaded to an Anker server.

What you can enter

Bed number and initials. There is no field for a name, an NHS number or a date of birth.

What’s collected

Anonymous crash and performance data only, so faults get fixed. Nothing you type is included.

Clearing it

Patients, jobs, notes and bleeps each clear separately from Settings at the end of a shift.

App data may be included in your iPhone’s own encrypted device backups. Full detail is in the privacy notice.

Our three principles.

Faster than pen and paper

No unnecessary features or complicated workflows. Just the things you actually need during a shift.
