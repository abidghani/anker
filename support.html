Anker Support

Anker is a fast, private notepad and task list for iOS, published by Boss Computing Ltd. Everything you enter is stored on your own device.

Get in touch

For help, bug reports or feature requests, email support@bosscomputing.com. We aim to reply within two working days. It helps if you include your iOS version and the version of Anker shown in Settings.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device, and nowhere else. There is no account, no sign-in and no sync. Nothing you type is sent to us and we cannot see it.

I lost my phone / reinstalled the app. Can you recover my notes?

No. Because nothing is ever sent to us, we hold no copy and have no way to restore anything. If your device backup included the app, restoring that backup may bring the data back — that is between you and Apple. Keep your own record of anything you cannot afford to lose.

Is my data included in my iCloud backup?

It may be, depending on your device settings. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple’s terms, not ours, and we have no access to them.

How do I delete a note?

Swipe the note in the list, or use the bin icon on the row. Notes with content ask for confirmation first.

How do I clear everything?

Open Settings from the gear icon and use the Clear options. Each section clears only its own data; Clear All Data removes everything. This cannot be undone.

Do I need an account?

No. Anker works fully from first launch with no sign-up.

What does the app send?

Anonymous technical telemetry only — crash reports, performance measurements and aggregate usage counts. No content you enter is ever included. See the Privacy Notice below.

The app is asking me to update

Install the latest version from the App Store. If you are offline, Anker will not block you.
